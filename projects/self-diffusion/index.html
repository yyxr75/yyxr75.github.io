Self-Diffusion for Solving Inverse Problems

Co-author · arXiv 2025

Self-diffusion alternates noising and denoising with an untrained convolutional self-denoiser. Because nothing is pretrained, the same procedure adapts to arbitrary forward operators — a general recipe for inverse problems where clean training data is scarce.

Highlights

  • Untrained convolutional self-denoiser
  • Works with arbitrary forward operators
  • Foundation for DeblurSDI
