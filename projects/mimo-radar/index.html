MIMO Radar Signal-Processing Chain

Vanjee Technology

Starting from the TI reference design, I built the signal-processing chain of an in-house MIMO radar toolkit: range and Doppler FFTs, detection in noise and clutter, and angle-of-arrival estimation from phase differences across the antenna array.

The hardware and software were deployed on an engineering sample unit and laid the groundwork for the radar–LiDAR fusion work that became RaLiBEV.

Highlights

  • Range and Doppler FFTs, CFAR detection
  • Angle-of-arrival estimation across the array
  • Deployed on an engineering sample unit
