Data Closed-Loop for Vehicle–Road Cooperative Perception

Vanjee Technology · team lead

Perception models for vehicle–road cooperative (V2I) driving meet conditions in the field that no training set anticipated. I led a three-person team building a data closed-loop system: collect real-world roadside sensor data, clean it and mine the hard cases, retrain, and redeploy.

Version 1.0 was rolled out to customers. Related roadside-LiDAR research appeared as SSP at the ITS World Congress 2023.

Highlights

  • Led a team of three engineers
  • Collect → clean & mine → retrain → redeploy
  • v1.0 shipped to customers
